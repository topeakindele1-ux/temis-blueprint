Meal prep

Cook once, eat four times

Most people don't need a new diet. They need three meals they can make without thinking and a shopping list that repeats. That's what meal prep actually is — not sixteen identical containers of sad chicken.

  • Pick two proteins and cook both at once
  • One carb that reheats well — rice, potatoes, pasta
  • Two vegetables, one raw and one roasted
  • A sauce. The sauce is what stops you getting bored
