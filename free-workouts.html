Free workouts

Start today, in your living room

Three workouts, free, no sign-up needed to read them. No gym, no equipment you don't already own. Do one of them this week and you'll know more about how you train than any quiz could tell you.

Before you start. These are general workouts, not personal medical advice. If you're pregnant, recovering from an injury or surgery, or managing a health condition, check with your doctor first. Warm up, move slowly, and stop if something hurts — sore is fine, sharp is not.

How to use them

Two a week beats seven for one week

Pick two of the three and do them on non-consecutive days. Keep the weight light enough that the last rep looks like the first. When a workout starts feeling easy, add a rep before you add weight.

  • Leave two days between the harder sessions
  • Stop two reps before you'd fail — that's where the progress is
  • Sore for a day or two is normal; sharp pain is not
  • Miss a week? Start again at the same weight, not from zero
