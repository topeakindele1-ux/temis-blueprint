Privacy policy

Last updated August 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Temi Blueprint collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: I collect as little as possible, I don't sell anything to anyone, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

Who is responsible

The Temi Blueprint, operated by Tope Akindele, operating as The Temi Blueprint, based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Contact: topeakindele1@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • If you join the email list: your first name and email address.
  • If you book a call: your name, email, chosen time, and anything you write in the booking form.
  • If you become a client: intake information including health history, injuries, medications, training history, and goals.
  • Automatically: basic, aggregated visit statistics from the site host. No advertising trackers and no third-party analytics cookies.

Why I collect it

To send you what you asked for, to schedule and deliver coaching, to build a plan that's safe for your body, and to run the business. I only use your information for the purpose you gave it to me for.

Health information gets extra care. Intake details are sensitive. They're used only to build and adjust your plan, they're never shared without your explicit written permission, and they're never used in marketing. Client stories and photos appear publicly only where the client has given specific written consent, and that consent can be withdrawn at any time.

Who else sees it

I use a small number of service providers to run the business. They only receive what they need to do their job:

  • Kit: email list and delivery
  • Cal.com: appointment booking and calendar
  • Cloudflare: website hosting
  • [Add your payment and course provider here once it's set up]

Some of these providers store data outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country. I don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone, ever.

How long I keep it

Email list details stay until you unsubscribe or ask me to delete them. Client records are kept for 7 years after our last session, for insurance and continuity reasons, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, ask me to delete it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days. Every marketing email also has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Booking and email forms may set functional cookies needed to work.

Children

This site and these services are not directed at children under 16, and I don't knowingly collect their information. Coaching for under-18s happens only with a parent or guardian involved.

Changes

If this policy changes I'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant and you're on the email list, I'll tell you.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please tell me first so I can fix it. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
