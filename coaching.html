What it's like

No punishment, no performance

I'm not going to shout at you, and I'm not going to pretend a plan is working when it isn't. Sessions are focused and friendly. You'll work hard, and you'll leave knowing exactly why we did what we did.

Coaching online works differently, and it's worth being clear about how. There's no weekly hour in a gym. You get your blueprint written out, you train it on your own schedule, and we speak on video regularly to look at what actually happened and change what needs changing. Some people prefer it — it fits around shifts, and nobody has to drive anywhere.

If I don't think I'm the right coach for what you need, I'll tell you on the first call and point you somewhere better.
