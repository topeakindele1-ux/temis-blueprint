How I measure it

The scale is the least interesting number

Weight moves for a dozen reasons that have nothing to do with whether the plan is working. These are the things I actually watch — and the things clients notice first.

  • Getting up off the floor without using your hands
  • Carrying the shopping in one trip
  • Sleeping through the night
  • The stairs at work stopping being an event
  • Cooking on a Tuesday when you're tired
  • Still training in month six

Being straight with you

What I won't promise

I can't promise you a number by a date. Results depend on your body, your history, your sleep, your stress and how much of the plan you actually do. What I can promise is a plan built for your real life, and honesty when something isn't working.
