Before we train

Client agreement

Please read this properly before signing. I'll go through it with you and answer anything that isn't clear.

1. What this is

This is an agreement between you and Tope Akindele, operating as The Temi Blueprint, for personal training and general nutrition coaching, in person in Vancouver, BC or online.

2. What I am, and what I'm not

I'm a fitness coach. I am not a doctor, physiotherapist, registered dietitian or any other regulated health professional. Nothing I provide is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and none of it replaces care from a qualified professional. If something you're dealing with needs one, I'll say so.

3. Exercise carries risk

Physical activity involves a genuine risk of injury. That includes muscle strains and tears, joint and ligament injuries, falls, fractures, heat exhaustion, and — rarely but seriously — heart attack, stroke or death. That risk exists even when a session is properly planned and properly supervised.

Some of that risk comes from things neither of us can foresee, including conditions you don't yet know you have.

4. You take that risk on knowingly

By signing, you confirm that you understand the risks set out above, that you're taking part voluntarily, and that you accept those risks — including risks that may result from ordinary negligence in how a session is planned or supervised.

5. Your health information

You confirm that you've completed the health screening honestly and completely, and that you have no condition that makes exercise unsafe for you. You agree to tell me right away if anything changes — a new injury, a new diagnosis, new medication, or a pregnancy.

If you answered yes to any question in part one of the screening, you confirm you have your doctor's clearance to train.

6. During sessions

You agree to work within your own limits, to follow instructions and technique cues, and to stop immediately and tell me if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, or shortness of breath. You're free to stop, rest or opt out of any exercise at any time, for any reason, without explanation.

7. Release

To the fullest extent permitted by law, you release Tope Akindele and The Temi Blueprint from claims for injury, loss or damage arising out of your participation, including claims arising from ordinary negligence. This does not release liability that cannot be released under British Columbia law, and does not cover gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

8. No guaranteed results

Results depend on your starting point, health history, sleep, stress, consistency and genetics. No specific outcome, timeline, weight or measurement is promised.

9. Sessions, payment and cancellation

In-person sessions are booked and paid for in blocks of eight, in advance. A block stays valid for twelve weeks from your first session.

Moving a session. Give me at least 24 hours' notice and we'll reschedule at no cost, as often as you need. A session cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed without telling me, is charged in full — that time was held for you and can't be given to anyone else at short notice.

When life happens. Illness, emergencies and genuinely bad weeks are not what that rule is for. Tell me what's going on and we'll sort it out. I'd far rather you rested than trained sick. If something serious means you need to stop for a while, tell me and I'll hold your remaining sessions.

Online coaching is billed monthly. You can cancel any time before your next billing date and you'll keep access to the end of the month you've paid for. I don't refund part of a month already started.

If I cancel — and occasionally I will — that session is rescheduled free, or refunded if we can't find a time that works.

10. Privacy

Your information is handled as described in the privacy policy. Health details are used only to build and adjust your plan, and are never shared without your written permission.
