Recipe

Overnight oats, done properly

5 minutes the night before · serves 1

Most overnight oats are bad, and they're bad for three specific reasons: too much liquid, no salt, and nothing with any texture. Fix those three and it's genuinely a breakfast you look forward to.

Yoghurt is doing the heavy lifting here. It brings tang and protein, and it stops the whole thing tasting flat.

What you need

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats (not instant)
  • ½ cup Greek yoghurt
  • ⅓ cup milk — start low, you can always add more
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tsp maple syrup or honey
  • A pinch of salt — do not skip this
  • Vanilla, if you have it
  • Berries, and something crunchy for the morning

How

Method

  1. Stir everything except the berries and the crunchy thing together in a jar.

  2. Taste it now, while you still can. It should taste slightly too sweet and slightly salty — it mellows overnight.

  3. Lid on, fridge, at least four hours.

  4. In the morning, add the berries and whatever's crunchy — toasted nuts, seeds, a spoon of granola. Add it now, not last night, or it goes soft.

Worth knowing

Notes and swaps

  • The salt is the whole trick. A pinch turns it from bland to actual food.
  • Make three jars on Sunday and breakfast is handled until Wednesday.
  • Add a scoop of protein powder with the milk if you're training in the morning.
