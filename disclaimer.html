Talk to your doctor first

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any new exercise or nutrition programme — particularly if you are pregnant or postpartum, have or are recovering from an injury or surgery, have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, an eating disorder, or any chronic health condition, or are taking medication that affects your exercise capacity.

Exercise carries risk

Physical activity involves a risk of injury. By using the workouts and guidance provided here, you accept that risk and take responsibility for your own safety. Work within your own limits, use a weight you can control, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or shortness of breath — and seek medical attention if it persists.
